About

FFmpeg is the first complete and free Internet Live Audio and Video Broadcasting solution. FFMpeg aims at being the command line tool to handle audio and video. It is a "three-in-one" solution. FFmpeg is capable of recording, encoding in many different formats simultaneously and streaming multimedia audio and video. FFmpeg generates streaming files, in many popular formats simultaneously, faster than any other solution.
