About

A simple multi-channel audio mixer.

What lin asks

Lin asks these before anything compiles, so you can walk away while it builds: which optional modules to use, and how to build it.

  1. flacfor MP3 encoders audio support
  2. mpg123for MPEG audio support
  3. libvorbisfor vorbis (OGG) audio support
  4. opusfilefor audio over the internet support
  5. libmodplugfor .mod audiofiles support
  6. fluidsynthfor MIDI software support, replaces built-in timidity
