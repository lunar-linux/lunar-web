About

The VideoLAN Client (VLC) is an MPEG, MPEG2, VCD and DVD player for Unix, Windows, MacOS X, BeOS and QNX. It can play MPEG streams from a file, from a network source, or directly from a VCD or DVD. There is video support for X11/XVideo, GGI, SDL, the Linux framebuffer device, DirectX, BeOS, and QNX, audio support for OSS, Esound, ALSA, and GTK+-2 interfaces.
