About

mpv is a fork of mplayer2 and MPlayer. It shares some features with the former projects while introducing many more.

What lin asks

Lin asks these before anything compiles, so you can walk away while it builds: which optional modules to use, and how to build it.

  1. lcms2for lcms2 support
  2. pulseaudiofor pulseaudio support
  3. jack2for jack audio support
  4. openalfor OpenAL audio support
  5. luajitfor lua support
  6. libdvdnavfor DVD support
  7. libvdpaufor libvdpau support
  8. libarchivefor archive support
  9. rubberbandfor time and pitch shifting support
  10. vapoursynthfor vapoursynth support
  11. libblurayfor Bluray DVD support
  12. libcdio-paranoiafor cdda support
  13. yt-dlpfor video-sharing websites playback
